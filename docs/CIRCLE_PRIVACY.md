# Circle privacy settings

Open **Circle → Account** to review your preferences. New preview accounts start with personalization, listening sharing and taste sharing on. Private session starts off. Existing accounts keep their saved choices.

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Personalized recommendations** | Whether Onda uses your meaningful listening and recommendation feedback to personalize your feed |
| **Share listening** | Whether followers can see your current or last-played track |
| **Share music taste** | Whether eligible followers can opt into your shared taste as a source for their own recommendations |
| **Private session** | Whether Onda temporarily pauses listening sharing and new taste-signal collection |

On another person's profile, **Hide listening activity** changes what you see from them across Circle. To change whether their taste influences your feed, use **Influence my recommendations** on the same profile.

When you block someone, Circle removes the active relationship and takes their activity out of view. Manage blocked profiles under **Account**.
